Someone, I say,
will remember us
in another time.

Sweet mother,
I cannot weave—
slender Aphrodite
has overcome me
with longing for a girl.

Evening—
you gather back
everything that
the bright dawn
scattered.

Love shook my heart,
like wind on the mountain
falling on oak trees.

For when anger spreads
in the breast,
guard the tongue
from barking foolish words.

I loved you once,
Atthis, long ago.

Moon has set,
and the Pleiades;
it is midnight,
the hours pass—
I lie alone.

Like the sweet apple
reddening on the high branch,
high on the highest branch—
forgotten by the pickers:
no, not forgotten,
but out of reach.
